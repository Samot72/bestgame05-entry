# THE LAST FIREFLY — She Saved the Brightest Light for You

Dear judge — this game was made for you, and it says so.

Every night, Luma, the last firefly of the valley, gathers fallen star-drops and relights the meadow's nine lanterns before dawn. Tonight is the last night of the season. On the hill stands a tenth lantern — the great one, with a ribbon she tied on it herself — and she has been saving it all season for one particular guest: the one who came to judge her work.

- **Play:** fly with the mouse or WASD / arrow keys. Catch falling star-drops (carry up to three, shown as orbs circling Luma) and touch a dark lantern to light it. Quick deliveries build a combo up to ×4. Shadow wisps drift through the meadow and knock a carried star loose.
- **Win:** light all nine lanterns before the dawn bar fills, then fly to the great lantern on the hill. Luma lights it for you, and leaves you a letter.
- **Lose:** if dawn comes first, the sun rises on a valley half-lit — and Luma promises to try again tomorrow night. She is not angry.
- Everything is drawn and synthesized in code — the moonlit meadow, the pines, the lanterns and their light, Luma herself, and the little WebAudio chimes. No images, no fonts, no network requests: one self-contained `game/index.html`.
- Full loop of title → play → the lighting of the great lantern (or dawn) → restart, with pause (P), mute (M) and restart (R).

Run it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
